Delete your Serg account

You can delete your Serg account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Serg and sign in.
  2. Customers: Home → scroll down → Delete my account. Vendors: dashboard → scroll down → Delete my account.
  3. Confirm twice. Your account is deleted straight away.

By email

Send an email to serg.weddings.app@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my Serg account”. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, plan and events, checklist and budget.
  • Your quote requests, chats and chat photos, saved vendors and reviews.
  • For vendors: the listing, photos, videos, documents and subscription receipts.

What is kept

  • If you have a booking that is still upcoming or ended less than 30 days ago, the app asks you to wait until then, so the other side keeps their record.
  • Records we must keep by law (for example payment records) are kept only as long as required and then deleted.
